# Northgate Harbour Survey, June 2026

Archived original. Do not edit.

The survey team visited Northgate Harbour on 14 June 2026 and recorded
conditions across the three basins.

Basin depth at mean low water was 4.2 metres in the outer basin, 3.1
metres in the middle basin, and 2.4 metres in the inner basin. The
outer basin has silted by roughly 15 centimetres since the 2024 survey.

Berth occupancy averaged 78 per cent across the season, with a peak of
94 per cent during the second week of August 2025.

The eastern breakwater shows spalling along a 40 metre stretch of its
seaward face. The team recommends inspection by a structural engineer
before the next winter storm season, but did not assess load bearing
capacity and makes no claim about it.

No measurements were taken of tidal flow rate. The team notes this as a
gap and recommends it for the next survey.
