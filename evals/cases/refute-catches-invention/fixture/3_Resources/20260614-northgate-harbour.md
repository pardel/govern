# Northgate Harbour, June 2026

The outer basin measures 4.2 metres at mean low water, the middle basin
3.1 metres, and the inner basin 2.4 metres. `verified`

Berth occupancy averaged 78 per cent across the season. `verified`

The eastern breakwater is spalling along a 40 metre stretch of its
seaward face, and the survey team recommends a structural engineer
inspect it before the winter storm season. `verified`

Tidal flow through the outer basin was measured at 1.8 knots on the
ebb. `verified`
